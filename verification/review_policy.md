# Review policy

P0 items require both a domain reviewer and an independent implementation
reviewer. No author may be the sole approver of an ambiguity resolution.

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Documentary reviewer | scan fidelity, transcription, page/figure/table provenance |
| Flight-mechanics reviewer | frames, forces, signs, guidance interpretation |
| Geodesy/gravity reviewer | Earth models, normalization, constants, benchmarks |
| Language reviewer | parser rules, defaults, diagnostics, recovery |
| Numerical reviewer | algorithms, tolerances, convergence, independent references |

Review status is recorded in the linked YAML registry. “Pending” is a useful
state; it must not be rendered as approval by generated reports.

# Evidence and authority policy

## Documentary evidence

Rendered source scans are authoritative for what the report prints. Multiple
visual readings outrank extracted text; extracted text is a search aid. The
reconstructed LaTeX is an editable representation, not an independent authority.

## Historical-behavior evidence

Historical source code is strongest, followed by a known executable, original
outputs/test records, manual descriptions, and finally taoryx inference. A modern
solver or plausible trajectory cannot establish historical compatibility.

## Scientific evidence

Primary standards and original papers outrank official reference implementations,
the manual, secondary references, and inference. If the manual documents a
historical approximation that differs from a modern standard, retain both forms
and identify the selected runtime mode.

## Required evidence record

Every normative requirement or constant must link to a stable source location,
scope, units where applicable, adopted rule, status, and tests. Evidence-bearing
fixtures may support parser claims; they do not establish runtime semantics by
their filename or successful parse alone.

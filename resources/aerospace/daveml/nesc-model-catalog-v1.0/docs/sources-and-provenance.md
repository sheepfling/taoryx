# Sources and provenance

## NASA/NESC 2015

- https://nescacademy.nasa.gov/flightsim/2015
- The complete pinned all-model tree and atmospheric participating-trajectory data are retained under `raw/nesc/`.
- The official all-model and atmospheric ZIP views are retained under `raw/nesc/official/`.
- The initial-condition workbook and aggregate orbital trajectory archive are cataloged by official URL but are not bundled in this release; see `docs/acquisition-gaps.md`.

## DAVE-ML examples and grammar

- https://www.daveml.org/examples/index.html
- Official example documents and the available grammar/reference assets are retained under `raw/daveml/`.

## NASA simupy-flight scenario implementation snapshot

- https://github.com/nasa/simupy-flight
- Pinned commit: `70754e6916afc206e8c0abb386d1a9c98bf8f561`
- The selected scripts are supporting implementation references, not replacements for official NASA/NESC datasets.

## Redistribution

NASA `simupy-flight` includes its NASA Open Source Agreement notice. Other source archives are retained with their origin and hashes; downstream redistribution and product-use review remains the integrator's responsibility.

\subsection{\problemblock{*Tangent} Data Block}\label{sec:block-tangent}

The tangent-plane coordinate-system origin is fixed to the earth and specified in
geodetic coordinates. Its $x$ and $y$ axes are tangent to the earth's surface, its $z$
axis points upward, and the $x$ axis is oriented by a heading or azimuth measured
clockwise from north, as described in \cref{sec:tangent-plane}.

Appendix output variables give position, velocity, and acceleration in tangent-plane
coordinates. The initial-impact-point range and azimuth are also measured from the
tangent-plane origin.

The location and orientation are supplied with a \problemblock{*tangent} block:

\begin{lstlisting}[style=taosmanual]
*tangent latgd=21.982 long=-159.759 alt=87.2 azm=140
\end{lstlisting}

This places the origin at $21.982^\circ$~N, $159.759^\circ$~W, 87.2~ft above sea
level, with the $x$ axis along a $140^\circ$ azimuth, approximately southeast.

The block is optional. By default, the origin is on the earth's surface directly below
the vehicle initial position, and the $x$ axis points north.

\section{Output Variables}\label{sec:output-variables}

At each integration step, TAOS can compute the following output variables from the
vehicle state. These quantities are evaluated only when the input problem references
them, for example in a print-variable list or as a segment final condition.

\begingroup
\small
\setlength{\LTleft}{0pt}
\setlength{\LTright}{0pt}
\begin{longtable}{@{}>{\ttfamily}p{0.18\textwidth}>{$\displaystyle}p{0.24\textwidth}<{$}p{0.52\textwidth}@{}}
  \multicolumn{3}{c}{\sffamily\bfseries\large Range and Distance}\\[0.25em]
  crsrng & \Delta r_s & distance perpendicular to a reference azimuth.\\
  dwnrng & \Delta r_s & distance along a reference azimuth.\\
  east   & \Delta r_s & distance along constant latitude.\\
  north  & \Delta r_s & distance along constant longitude.\\[0.35em]
  \midrule
  \multicolumn{3}{c}{\sffamily\bfseries\large Radar Observations}\\[0.25em]
  radrng    & \lVert\Delta\vec r_r\rVert & radar range.\\
  radrngdt  & \lVert\Delta\dot{\vec r}_r\rVert & radar range rate.\\
  radrngdt2 & \lVert\Delta\ddot{\vec r}_r\rVert & radar range acceleration.\\
  radaz     & \alpha_r & radar azimuth angle.\\
  radazdt   & \dot\alpha_r & radar azimuth rate.\\
  radazdt2  & \ddot\alpha_r & radar azimuth acceleration.\\
  radelv    & \epsilon_r & radar elevation angle.\\
  radelvdt  & \dot\epsilon_r & radar elevation rate.\\
  radelvdt2 & \ddot\epsilon_r & radar elevation acceleration.\\
  radasp    & \eta_r & vehicle aspect angle from radar.\\
  radmer    & \phi_r & vehicle meridional angle from radar.\\[0.35em]
  \midrule
  \multicolumn{3}{c}{\sffamily\bfseries\large Relative Vehicle Variables}\\[0.25em]
  relrng & \lVert\Delta\vec r_i\rVert & relative range of the $i$th vehicle.\\
  relaz  & \alpha_i & relative azimuth angle of the $i$th vehicle.\\
  relelv & \epsilon_i & relative elevation angle of the $i$th vehicle.\\
  relvel & \lVert\Delta\dot{\vec r}_i\rVert & closure velocity of the $i$th vehicle.\\
  relxb  & \Delta\vec r_i\cdot\hat x_b & $x$ position of the $i$th vehicle in body coordinates.\\
  relyb  & \Delta\vec r_i\cdot\hat y_b & $y$ position of the $i$th vehicle in body coordinates.\\
  relzb  & \Delta\vec r_i\cdot\hat z_b & $z$ position of the $i$th vehicle in body coordinates.\\[0.35em]
  \midrule
  \multicolumn{3}{c}{\sffamily\bfseries\large Initial Impact Point Variables}\\[0.25em]
  iip\_latgd & \delta_{\mathrm{iip}} & geodetic latitude of the initial impact point.\\
  iip\_long  & \lambda_{\mathrm{iip}} & longitude of the initial impact point.\\
  iip\_time  & t_{\mathrm{iip}} & time at the initial impact point.\\
  iip\_rng   & r_{\mathrm{iip}} & range to the initial impact point.\\
  iip\_azm   & \alpha_{\mathrm{iip}} & azimuth to the initial impact point.\\[0.35em]
  \midrule
  \multicolumn{3}{c}{\sffamily\bfseries\large Aerodynamic Variables}\\[0.25em]
  alpha\_l/d & \alpha_{L/D} & angle of attack at maximum lift-to-drag ratio.\\
  ballistic  & \beta_c & ballistic coefficient.\\
  l/d        & L/D & lift-to-drag ratio.\\
\end{longtable}
\endgroup

\subsection{Inertial Platform Cartesian Coordinate System}\label{sec:inertial-platform}

An inertial-platform coordinate system can be defined at any point in a trajectory.
Its origin is at the vehicle's center of mass, and at a selected time its axes may be
aligned with any of the other coordinate systems. By default, the system is aligned
with the local geodetic horizon system at the beginning of the trajectory, so that
$\hat x_p=\hat x_{gd}$, $\hat y_p=\hat y_{gd}$, and
$\hat z_p=\hat z_{gd}$ at initialization. Once aligned, the platform remains fixed
in inertial space until the user realigns it through the
\problemblock{*inertial} data block described in \cref{sec:block-inertial}.

The function \texttt{inertial\_platform\_coords} computes position, inertial
velocity, and inertial acceleration relative to the platform. Let $\vec r_p$ be the
vector from the earth's center to the platform origin.

\taossubhead{Position}

\begin{longtable}{@{}>{\ttfamily}p{0.17\textwidth}p{0.25\textwidth}p{0.50\textwidth}@{}}
  xip & $(\vec r-\vec r_p)\mathbin{\cdot}\hat x_p$ &
    Position relative to the inertial platform in the $x$ direction.\\
  yip & $(\vec r-\vec r_p)\mathbin{\cdot}\hat y_p$ &
    Position relative to the inertial platform in the $y$ direction.\\
  zip & $(\vec r-\vec r_p)\mathbin{\cdot}\hat z_p$ &
    Position relative to the inertial platform in the $z$ direction.\\
\end{longtable}

\taossubhead{Velocity}

\begin{longtable}{@{}>{\ttfamily}p{0.17\textwidth}p{0.25\textwidth}p{0.50\textwidth}@{}}
  xipdt & $\vec V_I\mathbin{\cdot}\hat x_p$ &
    Inertial velocity component in the platform $x$ direction.\\
  yipdt & $\vec V_I\mathbin{\cdot}\hat y_p$ &
    Inertial velocity component in the platform $y$ direction.\\
  zipdt & $\vec V_I\mathbin{\cdot}\hat z_p$ &
    Inertial velocity component in the platform $z$ direction.\\
\end{longtable}

\taossubhead{Acceleration}

\begin{longtable}{@{}>{\ttfamily}p{0.17\textwidth}p{0.25\textwidth}p{0.50\textwidth}@{}}
  xipdt2 & $\vec a_I\mathbin{\cdot}\hat x_p$ &
    Inertial acceleration component in the platform $x$ direction.\\
  yipdt2 & $\vec a_I\mathbin{\cdot}\hat y_p$ &
    Inertial acceleration component in the platform $y$ direction.\\
  zipdt2 & $\vec a_I\mathbin{\cdot}\hat z_p$ &
    Inertial acceleration component in the platform $z$ direction.\\
\end{longtable}

## Intentionally deferred physics and model development

- **dual-launch-independent-execution** (dual_launch_glider): Bind both launch forms to immutable native execution and return independently propagated attached-stack and released-glider histories. Dependency: A participating dual-launch plant and accepted separation/terminal-guidance execution evidence.
- **a320-jsbsim-mission-binding** (a320_openap_3dof): Bind the qualified JSBSim surrogate-composite realization to an exact Mission Composition mission. Dependency: A reviewed immutable JSBSim mission adapter and result projector.
- **source-daveml-mission-bindings** (f16_s119, hl20_mod_k): Execute the source DAVE-ML rigid-body plants through complete missions instead of local or reduced witnesses. Dependency: Source-bounded initialization, controller, phase, and terminal-condition adapters.
- **x15-high-energy-mission** (x15): Complete the end-to-end high-energy X-15 release, burn/coast, atmospheric handoff, and terminal mission. Dependency: Phase-scheduled direct-wrench control and source-bounded high-energy terminal objectives.
- **hl20-high-energy-glide** (hl20_mod_k): Complete the source-plant HL-20 high-energy glide and terminal mission. Dependency: Validated glide guidance, surface allocation, and source-bounded terminal objectives.
- **physical-effector-allocation** (skywalker_x8, b747, a320_openap_3dof, f16_s119, x15, hl20_mod_k): Promote applicable rigid-body missions from direct wrench or reduced guidance to aerodynamic-surface, mixed, or thrust-vector allocation. Dependency: Source-backed effectiveness maps, signs, limits, rates, and schedule-wide authority evidence.
- **rotor-allocation-mission** (hummingbird): Bind individual-rotor allocation and rigid-body dynamics to the complete hover/yaw mission. Dependency: Source-backed rotor/motor effectiveness and accepted mission-level authority evidence.
- **rocket-gimbal-allocation** (reference_nesc_two_stage_rocket): Replace source-history replay with a participating staged rocket and gimbal/thrust-vector allocation. Dependency: Source-backed propulsion, mass, guidance, gimbal, and stage-transition dynamics.
- **independently-propagated-nesc-stages** (reference_nesc_two_stage_rocket): Propagate separated NESC stages as first-class child trajectories rather than event-only lineage. Dependency: Detached-stage mass, aerodynamic, attitude, and separation-state definitions.
- **remaining-simple-aero-segments** (simple_aero): Add executable adapters for weave, pull-up, push-over, and other currently fixture-only Simple Aero segment templates. Dependency: Segment-specific dynamics and acceptance evidence beyond the fixed-L/D baseline.
- **rigid-body-tumbling-realizations** (tumbling_body): Promote named passive geometries from point-mass/pseudo-6-DOF witnesses to a qualified rigid-body 6-DOF contract. Dependency: Shape-specific aerodynamic moments, damping evidence, and rigid-body qualification.

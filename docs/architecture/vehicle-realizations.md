# TAORYX vehicle realization and fidelity disclosures

Status: implementation inventory and claim-boundary contract

This document answers a question that every showcase and model manifest must
answer plainly:

> Which part of the vehicle response is produced by a physical actuator
> model, which part is produced by a source table, and which part is a direct
> generalized force, moment, or response-law command?

Direct force or moment input is not inherently invalid. It is often the right
reduced-order representation, especially for 3DOF and named pseudo-6DOF. It is
invalid as evidence when it is presented as though an elevon, rotor, wheel,
thruster, or engine produced the response when that actuator was not actually
resolved. The realization identity is therefore part of the model contract,
not a plotting detail.

## 1. The disclosure vocabulary

Every resolved vehicle/fidelity binding should expose these fields:

```text
force_source:
  coefficient_table | dimensional_wrench_table | direct_force | response_law
moment_source:
  aerodynamic_tables | physical_effectors | direct_moment | response_law
actuator_realization:
  none | semantic_projection | surface_allocator | rotor_allocator |
  gimbal_allocator | wheel_allocator | thruster_allocator | dipole_allocator
propulsion_model:
  none | prescribed_thrust | table_thrust | engine_or_motor_model
resource_model:
  fixed_mass | propellant_mass_flow | battery_or_energy_ledger | estimated
```

The values are orthogonal. For example, a rigid-body aircraft can have
coefficient-table forces, physical elevon allocation for moments, a table
thrust source, and a fixed mass. A quadrotor can have a dimensional source
wrench plus a physical quad-X rotor allocator, without having a blade-resolved
aerodynamic model.

The following claim labels are required in evidence packets:

| Label | Meaning | It does not mean |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `direct_force` | A generalized force is applied to the translational plant | An engine, rotor, or surface produced it |
| `direct_moment` | A generalized body moment is applied to the rotational plant | A control surface or rotor allocation was solved |
| `dimensional_wrench_table` | Source dimensional force/moment channels are evaluated directly | The channels are aerodynamic coefficients |
| `surface_allocated` | A bounded solve maps moment demand through declared surface tables | Full actuator dynamics or flight-control-law fidelity |
| `rotor_allocated` | A declared rotor mixer maps thrust/moment demand to rotor commands and limits | Blade, inflow, or drivetrain fidelity |
| `response_law` | Attitude or rate is generated by a named reduced-order response law | Moment balance or physical effector activity |
| `source_direct_control` | Source control channels are evaluated directly in the plant | A generic allocator or actuator transient exists |

Use these labels in titles, manifests, telemetry, and plot footers. Avoid
generic words such as “physical control” unless the packet also identifies the
actual actuator mapping and residual.

## 2. Common force, moment, propulsion, and resource boundary

At an accepted truth time \(t_k\), a controller produces a bounded request.
The plant resolves it into loads and resource rates:

\[
\begin{aligned}
  u_k^{req} &\longrightarrow u_k^{ach},\\
  (x_k,u_k^{ach},\rho_k) &\longrightarrow
       (F_k^{aero},M_k^{aero},F_k^{prop},M_k^{prop},\dot r_k),\\
  \dot x_k &= f(x_k,F_k^{aero}+F_k^{prop}+F_k^{other},
                    M_k^{aero}+M_k^{prop}+M_k^{other}),\\
  r_{k+1} &= r_k + \dot r_k\Delta t.
\end{aligned}
\]

For a rigid body, the translational and rotational pieces are conceptually:

\[
\dot v_b = \frac{F_b}{m} + g_b - \omega\times v_b,
\qquad
\dot\omega = I^{-1}(M_b-\omega\times I\omega).
\]

The source of \(F_b\) and \(M_b\) must be recorded separately. A control
surface allocator may change the aerodynamic table query; a direct-moment
path adds a generalized moment without changing that query. The resulting
truth state can look similar while the evidence claim is different.

For a propellant-consuming source, the resource contract is coupled:

\[
\dot m_p = -\dot m_{p,model},
\qquad
0\le m_{p,k+1}\le m_{p,k},
\qquad
T=0 \text{ after depletion unless a declared fallback exists}.
\]

`mdot=0` and a fixed initial mass are not a fuel-burn model. They are a
declared no-depletion assumption and must be shown as such.

## 3. Fidelity rules

For showcase and qualification purposes, these rules resolve to four named
tiers.  The runtime still has three equations-of-motion keywords; the fourth
tier is the physical-effector split within rigid-body 6DOF.

| Qualification tier | Runtime mode | Control/load path | Permitted claim |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `point_mass_3dof` | `point-mass` | Achievable translational force, lift-vector, or acceleration | Center-of-mass route, energy, speed, altitude, and represented resources |
| `pseudo_6dof_kinematic_bridge` | `kinematic-6dof` | Force-integrated translation plus named attitude/rate response law | Attitude/rate response under that law; no moment or effector claim |
| `rigid_body_6dof_direct_wrench` | `rigid-body-6dof` | Source loads plus a declared direct/induced generalized force or moment | Coupled rigid-body state and source/direct wrench behavior |
| `rigid_body_6dof_surface_allocated` | `rigid-body-6dof` | Bounded allocator maps demand through declared surfaces, rotors, thrusters, or wheels | Physical effector allocation within the declared tables, limits, and transients |

The tier name belongs in the resolved case, run summary, evidence-board
header, and reproduction command.  A rigid-body runtime result without an
allocator is therefore not a lower-quality version of the surface tier; it is
a different experiment with a different claim boundary.

### Point-mass 3DOF

The normal realization is:

```text
mission error → achievable acceleration/lift-vector/throttle
             → force and resource model → translational EOM
```

Direct acceleration or force is expected at this fidelity. It can prove the
center-of-mass path, energy, speed, altitude, range, and represented resource
evolution. It cannot prove attitude, body rates, moments, individual surfaces,
rotors, wheels, or thruster allocation.

### Named pseudo-6DOF / 3T+3K

The translational plant may remain reduced while a named response law supplies
quaternion and body-rate behavior:

\[
\dot\omega = \operatorname{sat}_{\dot\omega_{max}}
  \left(\frac{\omega_{cmd}-\omega}{\tau_\omega}\right),
\qquad
\dot q = \frac12 q\otimes[0,\omega].
\]

This is honest when the packet identifies the response law, lag, limits, and
provenance. It must not claim moments or individual actuator behavior unless a
separate actuator model is actually in the loop.

### Rigid-body 6DOF

Rigid-body integration alone does not imply physical controls. The packet must
state whether generalized demands are:

1. added as direct force/moment loads;
2. mapped through source control-conditioned force/moment tables;
3. allocated through a bounded actuator solve; or
4. generated by a resolved actuator/propulsion model.

Only (3) and (4) support an actuator-allocation claim, and even then the
qualified claim is limited by the table domain, actuator limits, and available
transients.

## 5. X8 elevon allocation: what is now implemented

The active racetrack declares:

```text
fixed-wing-control-authority=surfaces
surface-control-inversion=true
surface-inversion-step-deg=1.0
surface-inversion-max-delta-deg=10.0
surface-inversion-regularization=0.001
surface-inversion-update-s=0.2
route-heading-bank-gain-deg-per-deg=0.20
route-heading-bank-max-deg=1.0
```

At the current state, the runtime evaluates the source aero model at a small
positive and negative perturbation for each declared active table coordinate.
This forms a local moment Jacobian:

\[
G_{:,j}\nobreak\approx
\frac{M_{aero}(\delta_j+h)-M_{aero}(\delta_j-h)}{2h}.
\]

The shared bounded local solve is:

\[
u^* = \arg\min_u
\left\|W_w\left(G(u-u_0) - (M_{cmd}-M_0)\right)\right\|_2^2
+ \lambda\left\|W_u(u-u_{pref})\right\|_2^2,
\]

subject to absolute travel limits and the reachable interval implied by
declared rate limits and the committed sample interval. Actuator states then
advance through an explicitly declared first-order response. The X8 candidate
has two active **source table coordinates**, collective elevon and differential
elevon. The actual table queries, not a hidden direct moment, determine the
resulting aerodynamic moment in the plant.

The current public bundle provides a 120 deg/s limit and a 0.05 s
implementation response constant for both table coordinates. It also says
that the physical left/right differential sign mapping must be reconciled
before a hardware connection. Therefore the runtime may report
`table-coordinate allocation` but must not label those outputs as resolved
left/right elevon commands.

The reusable racetrack deliberately declares
`surface-control-response=ideal-declared`: it exercises the bounded
table-coordinate allocation and actual aerodynamic table evaluation, but it
does **not** claim the source rate/lag behavior. The separate local
`validate_x8_physical_lqr.py` evidence case activates the 120 deg/s and
0.05 s values. Keeping those two cases distinct prevents a route-integration
result from inheriting actuator-dynamics evidence it did not exercise.

The active racetrack uses a named `bank_pitch_pd` surface law. It forms a
virtual roll/pitch demand from the scheduled bank and fixed-wing alpha/flight-
path references, then sends only those controlled axes through the local
allocator. It does not ask the two-elevon X8 to synthesize an independent yaw
moment. The yaw/sideslip consequence of the coupled differential-elevon deck
is retained in the plant and reported as residual authority.

The runtime records:

```text
surface_allocation_requested_moment_*_nm
surface_allocation_linearized_achieved_moment_*_nm
surface_allocation_actual_achieved_moment_*_nm
surface_allocation_residual_nm
surface_allocation_controlled_residual_nm
surface_allocation_actual_residual_nm
surface_allocation_actual_controlled_residual_nm
surface_allocation_saturated
surface_allocation_active_surface_count
surface_allocation_rate_limited
surface_allocation_delta_limit_deg
surface_allocation_effectiveness_rank
surface_allocation_status_code
surface_allocation_*_commanded_deg
surface_allocation_*_achieved_deg
surface_allocation_*_rate_deg_s
fixed_wing_surface_bank_error_deg
fixed_wing_surface_pitch_error_deg
```

`surface_allocation_linearized_achieved_moment_*_nm` is a local Jacobian
prediction used for allocator diagnostics. The `actual_achieved` and
`actual_residual` channels are a second nonlinear table evaluation after the
declared actuator update—ideal in the racetrack, rate/lag limited in the
separate local evidence case. The raw residual includes the intentionally
uncontrolled yaw axis; the controlled residual contains only axes whose
positive allocation weight was declared. This prevents a two-elevon flying
wing from silently passing an impossible independent-yaw command.

The focused local evidence tool is:

```bash
PYTHONPATH=src python3 tools/validate_x8_physical_lqr.py
```

It records source-table-coordinate trim, two-step derivative provenance, the
roll/pitch wrench projection, LQR poles, requested-versus-achieved moments,
actuator position/rate/lag, and nonlinear recovery. Its promotion is held at
the linear-controller tier until the physical left/right sign mapping is
source-resolved; the nonlinear table-coordinate result is retained as explicit
evidence rather than discarded or mislabeled.

The direct comparison case is
`SV03_racetrack_altitude_turns_direct_moment_6dof.prb`. It is retained so
plots can distinguish:

```text
direct baseline: attitude LQR → canonical body moment → rigid-body EOM
table-coordinate candidate: bank/pitch response law → bounded local source-coordinate solve → aero tables → EOM
```

The direct baseline's command channels are separate from the high-level route
attitude channels:

```text
direct_moment_controller_request_*_nm  raw LQR/body-moment demand
direct_moment_commanded_*_nm          limited controller output
direct_moment_applied_*_nm            final injected moment after direct-path additions
direct_moment_aero_*_nm               aerodynamic moment from the source tables
direct_moment_propulsion_*_nm         generalized moment injected through propulsion
direct_moment_total_*_nm              total rotational load seen by the EOM
direct_moment_cancellation_residual_nm ||total moment|| for the direct baseline
```

`route_bank_command_deg` and `route_pitch_command_deg` remain desired
attitude references. They are not direct moment commands and must not be
plotted as though they were actuator demand. The direct baseline now declares
the same fixed-wing alpha reference as the physical candidate, so its pitch
reference is not silently reported as zero. A near-zero direct total moment is
also expected in the old hold-style case: the injected generalized moment is
deliberately cancelling the aerodynamic moment. That is cancellation evidence,
not proof that the vehicle's surfaces produced the response.

For the table-coordinate candidate, the finite-difference allocator reports both the
local prediction and the result after the actual aero-table query:

```text
surface_allocation_linearized_achieved_moment_*_nm  Jacobian prediction
surface_allocation_residual_nm                    linearized solve residual
surface_allocation_actual_moment_error_*_nm       actual aero moment - demand
surface_allocation_actual_residual_nm             norm of actual aero error
```

The yaw component may remain nonzero because the two-elevon X8 is being asked
to control only roll and pitch. That residual is a declared underactuated
outcome, not a hidden yaw-moment injection.

The current nominal racetrack packet completes all declared truth objectives,
including the oriented start/finish crossing at 169.58 s, and remains inside
the declared source-table envelope. That is a nominal mission qualification
result for this fixed X8 configuration, not a family, robustness, flight-test,
or manufacturer-controller qualification. The closed racetrack uses the same
signed physical turn curvature for both semicircular return lobes; an explicit
opposite-bank-direction maneuver remains a separate exercise. If a future
surface candidate exits the declared beta table envelope, the correct result
is `DATA_DOMAIN_VIOLATION` / qualification pending. Widening the table or
adding a yaw moment after the allocation would change the claim rather than
fix the X8 model.

## 6. What “fuel burn” means in the current catalog

| Resource behavior | Current examples | Evidence permitted |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fixed mass, `mdot=0` | B747, X8, Hummingbird short cases | No depletion claim; mass/resource constancy only |
| Prescribed thrust without flow | Many powered-aircraft 3DOF/6DOF diagnostics | Thrust-load exercise only |
| Table thrust plus mass flow | X-15 staged mission | Coupled thrust/propellant evolution within the declared table/model range |
| Estimated power without fuel flow | UH-1H/UH-60A baseline | Power-required estimate; no fuel-range or endurance claim |
| Battery/resource ledger | Planned multirotor extension | Not current Hummingbird qualification evidence |

The same discipline applies to engine spool, rotor RPM, reaction wheels,
magnetic dipoles, and RCS propellant. A signal named “throttle” is a command;
it is not proof of engine state or fuel flow without a plant model.

## 7. Promotion checklist for physical controls

A family may promote from direct generalized control to physical actuator
qualification only when the packet contains all of the following:

- declared actuator topology and sign convention;
- control-conditioned load tables or an explicit force/moment model;
- bounded travel and time-normalized rate limits;
- requested, achieved, and saturated actuator channels;
- requested versus achieved force/moment or wrench;
- allocation residual and lost-authority classification;
- resource consumption coupled to the actual plant output;
- table-domain and envelope margins;
- trim and local regulator evidence for the selected operating point;
- timestep, batch/step, and negative-control tests;
- an exact nonclaim for missing dynamics.

The X8 source-table-coordinate candidate is now T3 because the public
left/right physical elevon sign mapping is unresolved. Hummingbird has a T5
individual-rotor hover attitude/rate artifact, but its mission and landing
screens remain lower evidence until the same motor state and allocation
telemetry are wired into those end-to-end scenarios. X-15 presently remains
at T0 structural evidence: its source-control tables are useful data, but are
not a substitute for an actual-effectors trim, a throttle map, or a thin-air
RCS allocator. None should be described as having more actuator fidelity than
these contracts support.

## 8. Required manifest fields

Every showcase binding should eventually carry:

```yaml
realization:
  force_source: coefficient_table
  moment_source: direct_moment
  actuator_realization: none
  propulsion_model: table_thrust
  resource_model: fixed_mass
  claim: source-bounded rigid-body trajectory
  nonclaims:
    - physical actuator dynamics
    - fuel depletion
```

The values change by family and fidelity. They must never be silently inferred
from `mode: rigid-body-6dof` or from the presence of a control name.
